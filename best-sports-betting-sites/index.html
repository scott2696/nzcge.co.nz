Three of these four offers have a clearly positive expected value. Across the fifteen casino welcome bonuses we track, only two do.

The explanation is in the economics. A casino makes its money from the house edge on every spin, so a 40x wagering requirement is not really an obstacle it wants you to clear — the bonus is an acquisition cost it expects to recover through the turnover itself. A sportsbook makes its money from the overround on the bets you place, so a 5x requirement at a minimum price of 1.80 still produces the volume it needs, and it can afford to let you clear it.

The exception on this page proves the rule. Gunsbet’s 285% to NZ$14,700 at 40x is a casino bonus wearing a sportsbook’s jersey, and it prices like one: NZ$8,000 of turnover on a NZ$200 claim, at an expected cost of roughly NZ$400. The headline is the biggest on the page and the value is the worst.

The two conditions to check on any free bet

  1. The minimum odds. 1.80 or 2.00 on these offers. It exists to stop you clearing the requirement on odds-on shots with almost no variance, and it is the condition that makes the offer work for the book.
  2. Whether the stake is returned. A NZ$100 free bet at 3.00 that returns NZ$200 (winnings only) is worth considerably less than one returning NZ$300 (stake plus winnings). Almost all free bets are stake-not-returned; the offers here are matched deposits rather than pure free bets, which is better.

Sports betting sites NZ — common questions

Which is the best sports betting site for New Zealanders?

Among offshore books, Rooster Bet at 9.0 — sharpest all-round package, NZD banking, a free bet at 6x rather than 40x, Dama N.V. named behind it, and a genuine 6,000-game casino attached. But the answer for most New Zealanders is TAB NZ plus one offshore account: TAB NZ for domestic racing, NPC and netball where it is both lawful and better priced, and an offshore book for the NBA, European football and in-play where it is not.

Is it legal to use these sportsbooks from New Zealand?

Using one is not an offence for you. Since 28 June 2025 only TAB NZ and its partner may lawfully offer or promote racing and sports betting to a person in New Zealand, and the Racing Industry Amendment Act 2025 expressly provides that an individual cannot be convicted for placing a bet with an offshore operator. The prohibition is on supply. The full position, with what you give up in exchange, is on online betting NZ.

Why are the sportsbook welcome offers so much better than casino bonuses?

Because the economics are different. A casino’s margin is the house edge on every spin, so it can afford to set a 40x requirement it knows almost nobody will clear — the bonus is an acquisition cost it mostly recovers. A sportsbook’s margin is the overround on the bets you actually place, so a 5x or 6x requirement at a minimum price still generates the turnover it needs. The result is that three of the four offers on this page have a positive expected value, which is true of only two of the fifteen casino offers we track.

Can I bet in New Zealand dollars?

At Rooster Bet and Bet&Play, yes. Gunsbet and Ivibet hold euros, which costs you about 4.5–5% on the round trip in conversion — enough to outweigh a pricing advantage on a regular punter’s annual turnover. TAB NZ is New Zealand dollars by definition.

What is in-play betting and which book does it best?

Betting on a match while it is in progress, with prices updating continuously. Bet&Play is the best of these four, and the reason is suspension time: on a Premier League fixture we tracked, its next-goal market was suspended for about four minutes across ninety, against over twelve minutes at two rivals. Every minute of suspension is a minute you cannot bet, and it usually falls exactly when the price is moving.

Do these sportsbooks cover New Zealand sport?

Patchily, and it is the honest weakness of all of them. All Blacks tests and Super Rugby are priced competitively because they are globally traded. The NPC is priced at a wider margin than TAB NZ. The Heartland Championship is mostly not priced at all. Netball is quoted only by Ivibet, at 110–112%. For New Zealand domestic sport, TAB NZ is generally the better bet on price as well as being the lawful one.

How fast do these bookmakers pay out?

From our timed testing: Bet&Play 2–8 hours on crypto, Rooster Bet a median of 3 hours 20 minutes, Ivibet 2–12 hours. Gunsbet does not support crypto at all, so its fastest option is an e-wallet at 12–24 hours. Weekly caps range from NZ$8,000 at Rooster Bet down to €4,000 — about NZ$7,840 — at Gunsbet.

People also ask

What is the best betting site in NZ?
TAB NZ is the only authorised operator and the best option for domestic racing and NZ competitions. Among offshore books, Rooster Bet scores highest on our weights.
Are betting bonuses worth it in New Zealand?
Sportsbook offers usually are — 5x or 6x turnover requirements are clearable at a profit. Casino-style 40x offers attached to a sportsbook are not.
Can I bet on NPC rugby online?
Yes, at TAB NZ at an overround around 106.9%, and at some offshore books at around 108.3%. TAB NZ is the better price as well as the lawful option.
Which bookmaker has the best odds on the All Blacks?
Offshore books average about 1.6 percentage points better than TAB NZ on test match odds, because the All Blacks are a globally traded market.
Do offshore bookmakers accept NZ bank cards?
Often, but declines are increasingly common as New Zealand banks block gambling merchant codes. Bank transfer, e-wallets and crypto are more reliable.
What is an each-way bet in New Zealand?
Two bets in one: a win bet and a place bet of equal stake. Common on racing. At TAB NZ the place dividend comes from the tote pool; at an offshore book it is a fraction of the fixed win odds.
