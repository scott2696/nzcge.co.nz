What to check before joining a new casino

  1. Find the operating company in the footer

    A named entity with a registration number — Rabidi N.V., Dama N.V., Vertikal N.V. A licence badge with no company behind it is the weakest signal in this market.

  2. Verify the licence on the regulator's register

    Curaçao Gaming Control Board, Anjouan, Tobique — each publishes one. Confirm the number resolves to the company named on the site, not just that a licence exists.

  3. Read the withdrawal terms before the bonus terms

    Weekly cap, minimum cashout, and whether a pending withdrawal can be cancelled. These decide what a win is actually worth; the bonus decides what the marketing looks like.

  4. Check for a maximum cashout on bonus winnings

    New casino sites lean on large headline percentages. A 600% offer capped at 10x the bonus is a much smaller product than it appears.

  5. Search the brand plus 'withdrawal' or 'complaint'

    Not the brand alone, which returns affiliate pages. A brand with no complaint record at all is usually a brand with no history — which for a new casino is expected, and worth knowing.

  6. Deposit the minimum, then withdraw before you commit

    Play a little, request a cashout, see what happens. This is exactly what we do, 214 times, and it tells you more than any review.
