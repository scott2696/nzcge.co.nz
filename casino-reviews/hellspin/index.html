What a well-run tournament programme looks like

Slot tournaments are common and mostly badly run. The usual pattern: a prize pool announced without the rules, a leaderboard that updates hourly if at all, and qualification criteria you discover after you have failed to meet them.

Hellspin publishes the prize pool and the distribution before the event opens, runs a leaderboard that updates in near real time, and states the qualifying games and minimum stake up front. Events run most days, with larger weekend tournaments.

The scoring is usually highest-multiplier-on-a-single-spin rather than total win, which matters: it means a small stake can place, and the tournament is not simply a leaderboard of whoever wagered most.
