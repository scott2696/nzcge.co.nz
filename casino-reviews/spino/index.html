The tax point nobody else makes

Casual gambling winnings are not taxable income in New Zealand. Cryptocurrency is treated by Inland Revenue as property, and disposing of property acquired for the purpose of disposal is taxable.

So: your win is not taxed, but if you buy USDT at one NZD rate, gamble, and later convert back at a higher one, the gain on the crypto itself can be taxable. The two questions are separate and almost every competitor page collapses them into 'gambling winnings are tax-free in NZ'. We work a full example on the tax page.
