The disclosure problem, stated plainly

We looked for licensing information on Roby Casino in the footer, the terms and conditions, the about page and the help centre. There is none. No regulator named, no licence number, no operating company, no registered address.

Every other operator we rank publishes at least a regulator, and thirteen of the nineteen also name the operating company. This is not a technicality. It is the difference between a complaint you can escalate and one you cannot.

Here is what it means in practice. If a withdrawal is refused at Spinjo, you can take it to the Curaçao Gaming Control Board, naming Rabidi N.V. There is a register, a process and a body with the power to act on the licence. At Roby, there is an email address.

We are not going to pretend the testing went badly, because it did not. Six timed withdrawals, all paid, all within the advertised windows. Our concern is not that Roby has failed anyone we know of. It is that if it did, there would be no recourse — and you cannot tell in advance which side of that line you will end up on.
