Why the minimum deposit is not a trivial detail

A NZ$10 minimum sounds like marketing. It has a practical consequence: it sets the smallest amount you can have at risk at one time, and at most sites on this page that floor is NZ$20 to NZ$35.

More to the point, many sites set a bonus-eligible minimum well above the deposit minimum. Deposit NZ$10 and you get NZ$10 — the match needs NZ$30. Lucky Circus does not do that. The welcome bonus is live from the NZ$10 floor.

There is also no withdrawal minimum above the deposit floor, which sounds obvious and is not. At two sites we tested, a NZ$35 balance could not be withdrawn because the minimum cashout was NZ$50 — money you can only ever gamble is not really your money.

35x, in dollars

The market standard is 40x. Lucky Circus is 35x. On a NZ$100 bonus that is NZ$3,500 of turnover instead of NZ$4,000 — NZ$500 less, or about NZ$20 of expected cost at 96% RTP.

Small in absolute terms, and it points the right way. Very few operators move off 40x, and the ones that do — Lucky Circus at 35x, Kingdom at 30x, Smash at 10x on deposit plus bonus — are the ones whose bonuses are worth reading.

The NZ$5,000 weekly withdrawal cap is low. On a small-stakes account that will never matter; if you hit a NZ$25,000 jackpot it will take five weeks to collect.
