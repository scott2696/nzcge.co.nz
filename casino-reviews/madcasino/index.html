The sportsbook, priced honestly

We audit margins weekly against TAB NZ. On an All Blacks test, MadCasino's two-way market ran an overround of about 104.2% — competitive, and usually a shade better than TAB NZ's fixed odds on the same market. On an NPC fixture the overround widened to around 108%, which is poor.
